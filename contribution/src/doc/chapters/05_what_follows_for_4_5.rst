What follows for §4.5
=====================

Three recommendations, each derived from the run rather than from principle.

First, publish the distribution. A mean of 69.3 over a range of 62 to 76 and a mean of 69.3 over a range of
40 to 95 describe different instruments; the single number cannot tell them apart.

Second, retire dimensions that do not vary in the population being measured, or restate them so they do. A
dimension scoring 5.0 for every item is a description of the population, not an assessment of the item.

Third, keep the minimum in the aggregate. Five of twelve items passed or failed on the weakest dimension
rather than on the average, which is the behaviour wanted in a gate: a document with one unstated field is
not made acceptable by five well-written sections.
