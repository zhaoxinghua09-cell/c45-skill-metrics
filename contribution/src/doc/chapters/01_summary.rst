Summary
=======

A metric is useful only where it discriminates. This contribution reports a six-dimension score applied to
twelve agent skills on a fixed date, and draws one conclusion from the result: on this population three of the
six dimensions returned the same value for every item - two at the maximum, one at a ceiling the sample
imposed - so none of the three measured a difference, and the ranking was carried
entirely by the remaining three. The note states what the score can support, and proposes that any metric
offered to the group be accompanied by its distribution rather than by its mean.
