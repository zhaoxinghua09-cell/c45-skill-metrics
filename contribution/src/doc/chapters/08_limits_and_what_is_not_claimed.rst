Limits and what is not claimed
==============================

Twelve items, one date, one tool, one operator's collection. The scores measure conformance to stated rules
about document form; they do not measure whether an artifact is useful, correct or safe, and a high score is
not evidence of any of those. The threshold of 70 is the tool's default and is not justified by this sample;
it is reported because it was used, not because it is right.
